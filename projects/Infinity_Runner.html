About

A fast-paced endless runner game where you dodge obstacles and collect points.

Game Summary

Game

Player keeps running until it is colliding with any obstacles. It can increase health and points if it collects health packs and coins respectively. The game speed increases if the player can collect thunder strike.

Technologies & Tools Used in MechaMorph

  • 🎮Game Engine & Programming: Unity (3D engine) C# (scripting language)
  • 🛠️ Development Workflow: GitHub (version control)

Development Information

How to Play

  • Press Space to Jump
  • Press 'R' to Restart the game
  • Press 'Esc' to Pause/Resume the game
  • Press 'Q' to Quit the game
  • Collect Health to get more chances
  • Collect Dollar to increase points

Controls

  • Move Left: A or Left Arrow
  • Move Right: D or Right Arrow
